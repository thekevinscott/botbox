Added `Botbox.run(keep=name)` to retain the agent container and its socket volume after exit, cancellation or timeout. `RunResult.container_name` reports the retained name. `Botbox.restart(name, command, ...)` continues from the kept filesystem under a fresh lockdown. A restart into the same `output` writes numbered files (`stdout.1.txt`, `proxy.1.log`) and keeps earlier attempts'.
