**Changed** (breaking): the agent's home lives in its container, not in a host directory. The harness images create it, open to any uid, and nothing is mounted over it. `Agent.stage_auth` takes a new `HomeFiles` and writes into it; the files reach the container as a gzipped tar at the head of its stdin, and the relay unpacks them into the home at start. Only their size is in the env, as `BOTBOX_HOME_BYTES`. The run's temp directory now holds only streams and logs.
